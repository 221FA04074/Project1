Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register a new resident?

To register a new resident, go to the "Residents" section, click on "Add New Resident," and fill in the necessary information.

How do I contact support?

You can contact support by clicking on the "Contact Support" button below or calling our support line at +91 9392630070, +91 7989026236

How can I update resident information?

To update resident information, go to the resident's profile, click on "Edit," and make the necessary changes.

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to "Settings," click on "Reset Password," and follow the instructions provided.
